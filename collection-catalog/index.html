Collection Catalog

Privacy Policy & Support — last updated 7 September 2026

Collection Catalog is an app for cataloguing personal collections — collectible items with photos, values, condition, storage locations, and notes, organised into collections. This page explains how the app handles your information and how to get help.

What the app stores, and where

  • Everything you enter — item names, photos, estimated values, asking prices, valuation sources, purchase details, storage locations, condition, status, notes, your collections, and the "Report Info" you optionally fill in (name, business name, address, phone, email) — is stored on your device.
  • If you turn on "Store in iCloud" in Settings, that same data is synced through your own iCloud account so it is available on your other devices. This uses Apple's iCloud infrastructure and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to your iCloud data. iCloud sync is off by default.

What leaves your device

  • Nothing automatically. The app has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits.
  • Data leaves your device only when you choose to export or share it — a PDF report, a CSV spreadsheet, a "calling card" image, or a backup file — and then it goes only where you send it (Messages, Mail, Files, AirDrop, and so on).

Camera and photo library

The app requests camera access so you can photograph items and scan documents (certificates, receipts, grading slips), and photo library access so you can attach existing photos. Images are stored with your catalogue as described above. The app does not access your photo library for any other purpose.

Data collection

The developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share any of your data. There is no analytics or crash-reporting service in the app.

Children

Collection Catalog is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
