Aud-It Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

Aud-It ("the app") is an audit and inspection tool developed by Chris Bazzie. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

What the app collects

The app generates a random device identifier, stored in your device's Keychain, the first time you open it. This identifier is sent to our backend (Google Firestore) along with the access code you enter, solely to verify that your device is authorized to use the app. No name, email address, or other personal information is collected or required to use the app.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No sharing of data with third parties for marketing purposes
  • No account creation, password, or login required

Your audit data (entries, notes, photos)

Everything you create in the app — audit entries, notes, and photos — is stored locally on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, that data is stored in your own private iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud Documents service. We do not have access to this data; it is not uploaded to our servers.

Data retention

The device identifier remains bound to your access code for as long as you use the app. If you get a new device or want a code released, contact us using the information below and we'll clear the binding.

Children's privacy

Aud-It is a professional inspection tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
