Aud-It Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Aud-It ("the app") is an audit and inspection tool developed by Chris Bazzie, available on iOS and Android. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used, on either platform.

What the app collects

The app generates a random device identifier the first time you open it — stored in your device's Keychain on iOS, or using Android's built-in device identifier (ANDROID_ID) on Android. This identifier is sent to our backend (Google Firestore) along with the access code you enter, solely to verify that your device is authorized to use the app. No name, email address, or other personal information is collected or required to use the app.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No sharing of data with third parties for marketing purposes
  • No account creation, password, or login required

Your audit data (entries, notes, photos)

Everything you create in the app — audit entries, notes, and photos — is stored locally on your device by default.

  • On iOS, turning on iCloud sync in Settings stores that data in your own private iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud Documents service.
  • On Android, turning on Data Sync in Settings lets you choose a folder — in Google Drive, Dropbox, or any other storage location your device supports — where the app keeps a sync file. The app uses Android's own system file picker to do this; it never sees or stores your account credentials for that service.

Either way, we do not have access to this data — it is never uploaded to our own servers.

Photos, scanning, and location

When you attach a photo, scan a document, or scan a barcode or QR code, the app uses your device's camera (or the system's built-in scanner) and keeps the resulting images in your audit data on your device. On Android the document and barcode scanners run on your device through Google Play services. If a checklist asks for your location, the app reads your device's location only at that moment, after you grant permission, and saves it in that entry on your device. Photos, scans, signatures, and locations are never sent to us.

Checklists

The app can download checklists that have been published for you. This is a read-only request to our backend (Google Firestore) for the checklist text; it sends no audit data. As with any internet request, your device's IP address is visible to the servers that handle it. Your answers to a checklist are saved as an ordinary entry in your audit data on your device.

Data retention

The device identifier remains bound to your access code for as long as you use the app. If you get a new device or want a code released, contact us using the information below and we'll clear the binding.

Children's privacy

Aud-It is a professional inspection tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy
