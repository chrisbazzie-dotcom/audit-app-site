Checklister Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Checklister ("the app") is a survey and checklist app developed by Chris Bazzie, available on iOS and Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

What the app collects

We do not collect any data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and we run no servers that receive data from it. Surveys you load, answers you give, photos, signatures, and locations are stored on your device only.

Where your answers can go

When you finish a survey, the survey itself may tell the app to send your answers somewhere: to a web address (a webhook) or to an email address. That destination is set by whoever wrote the survey, not by us, and the data goes directly from your device to that destination. Please check where a survey sends its results before you submit it. The app also lets you share or export a survey's results yourself, for example as a CSV or PDF file, using your device's own share sheet or mail app. We never receive a copy.

Camera, photos, scanning, and location

When a survey asks for a photo, a scan, or a barcode or QR code, the app uses your device's camera, or on Android the system scanner provided by Google Play services, and your photo library if you choose a photo from it. If a survey asks for your location, or asks you to stamp a photo with where it was taken, the app reads your device's location only at that moment, after you grant permission. A survey can also stamp a photo with the date and time, using the photo's capture time or a trusted time from the internet (see below). Photos, scans, signatures, and locations stay in your survey answers on your device unless a survey sends them on as described above.

NFC tags

If you tap an NFC tag, the app reads the tag's ID, or the survey link written on it, on your device. A survey can record that ID, the time, and your location as a check-in saved with your answers. The app can also write survey links onto tags you hold. Tag data is never sent to us.

Trusted time

To make date and time stamps trustworthy, the app may ask a public time server for the current time (on iOS, captive.apple.com; on Android, connectivitycheck.gstatic.com). This request contains none of your data, but, as with any internet request, your device's IP address is visible to that server.

Children's privacy

Checklister is a professional tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
