Checklister Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Checklister Studio ("the app") is an app for building surveys and checklists, developed by Chris Bazzie for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

What the app collects

We do not collect any data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and we run no servers that receive data from it. The surveys you create are saved as files on your device, or in a location you choose such as iCloud Drive, using the system's own file handling. We cannot see them.

Sharing and exporting

You can share a survey as a file or as a QR code. That happens only when you choose to, through your device's share sheet or by showing the code on screen. A survey you build can name a web address or an email address where its answers will be sent when someone completes it; the app only writes that setting into the survey and sends nothing itself.

Testing a survey

The Test tab runs your survey inside the app so you can try it. Depending on the questions, this can use your camera, your photo library, your location, or the internet to fetch a trusted time, exactly as the Checklister app does, and asks for your permission first. Test answers stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Cloud-connected builds

A separate build of Checklister Studio for a single organization can sign in to that organization's own cloud account to open and save its surveys there. That build is not the version published for the general public. Data in it goes only to the organization's own account, not to us.

Children's privacy

Checklister Studio is a professional tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
